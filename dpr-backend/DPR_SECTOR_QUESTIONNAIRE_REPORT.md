# DPR Sector Questionnaire Report 📋🌾

## 1. Sector-Specific Question Resolution Matrix
- **Food Processing**: Product category, daily/monthly capacity, processing technology, FSSAI status, cold chain requirements, shelf life, raw material seasonality.
- **Textiles & Garments**: Fabric type, GSM, stitching capacity, number of looms, raw material yarn suppliers, quality control, domestic/export split.
- **Engineering / CNC**: Sub-micron tolerance, 5-axis CNC machinery, quality certifications, raw material alloys, waste recycling.
- **Agriculture & Allied**: Crop type, land acreage, irrigation source, yield estimates, fertilizer inputs, seasonal risk mitigation.
- **IT / ITES / SaaS**: Tech stack, subscription pricing, TAM/SAM market size, MRR/ARR targets, cloud infrastructure costs.
