# DPR Research, Scheme & Risk Report 📊🔬

## 1. Intelligence Subsystem Verification
- **Research Engine**: Retrieves targeted growth stats per sector/geography (e.g., Food Processing 12.8% CAGR, CNC Manufacturing 14.5% CAGR).
- **Scheme Engine**: Matches relevant government schemes (PMEGP, PMFME, MUDRA, KVIC) based on sector, category, and loan size.
- **Risk Engine**: Identifies sector-specific operational risks (Raw Material Volatility, Cold Chain Failure, Machinery Downtime, Attrition) with severity and mitigation matrices.
